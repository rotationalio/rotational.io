description: | 
  Many valuable business workflows depend on documents, forms, PDFs, images, screenshots, and other unstructured inputs. Multimodal AI can help, but only if teams can evaluate quality, manage cost, preserve traceability, and deploy the workflow reliably.

  **What will you learn in under 30 minutes?**

  - OpenRouter offers 288 image-capable models and 177 models that process documents such as PDFs.
  - Using business receipts, Patrick used <a href="https://rotational.ai" target="_blank">Endeavor</a> to customize and compare model-and-prompt combinations against the same examples.
  - He then deployed the best-performing workflow as a governed API endpoint, ready for use across the organization.

  **Why should you care?**

  - Avoid vendor lock-in: Compare models and prompts as capabilities and costs change.
  - Scale document automation: Reuse the approach across any custom document-heavy workflows like RFPs.
  - Move AI from pilot to production with control: Test, evaluate, and deploy workflows through one platform with full control.
